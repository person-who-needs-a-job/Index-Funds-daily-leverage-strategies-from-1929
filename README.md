# S&P 500 Bull–Bear Market Regime Transition Analysis

A quantitative study of S&P 500 bull–bear market regime transitions from 1950 to the present.The project uses the 200-day moving average, historical event analysis, momentum, volatility, drawdown, and Federal Reserve policy conditions to examine whether major market regime shifts can be distinguished from short-term market noise.The goal is not to predict the exact date of a market turning point, but to develop transparent and interpretable signals for identifying potential regime transitions and managing market exposure.
